Valhalla

A beloved personal project that allowed me to bring brand identity into a physical space. In a market full of dark, rock-inspired beer shops, Valhalla stood out through a "Viking-meets-Comic" aesthetic. I oversaw everything visual: from the logo and growler labels to the hand-painted mural on the storefront.

While my career has centered on digital products, my foundation is in Graphic Design. Valhalla was a personal venture, a craft beer refill station born from a passion for local brewing culture. As a co-founder, I was responsible for the entire visual ecosystem, from the core branding and digital collateral to the hand-painted storefront mural and packaging labels.

At the time, the local craft beer scene was dominated by a "Rock & Roll / Industrial" aesthetic. We chose to pivot away from the status quo, carving out a unique market position by blending Viking mythology with a vibrant, comic-inspired color palette. Though the project was cut short by the 2020 pandemic, it remains a favorite case study in using bold, unconventional world-building to stand out in a saturated market.
