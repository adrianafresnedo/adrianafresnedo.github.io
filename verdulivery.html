Verdulivery

Verdulivery is a small business dedicated to providing delivery services for fresh produce and grocery items. They required to explore a way to distribute their product list effectively.

Project Overview

Verdulivery serves a diverse clientele, ranging from busy professionals to elderly individuals with specific accessibility needs. Their unique value proposition is a deeply personalized service model. Originally, the business shared product lists manually via WhatsApp. However, as their inventory expanded beyond produce to general groceries, they required a more scalable way to manage and distribute their catalog without losing their signature human touch.

  • Role: Designer & Frontend Developer
  • Technologies: Wordpress, CSS, HTML5
  • Duration: 1 month
  • Year: 2022

The Challenge

The project was defined by several strategic constraints:

  • Technical Readiness:The business was not yet ready to manage or maintain a full-scale e-commerce infrastructure.
  • Lean Budget: The solution needed to be cost-effective and high-impact.
  • Digital Literacy: The user base ranged from tech-savvy individuals to those with limited digital experience, requiring a high bar for accessibility.
  • Relationship Management: It was vital to maintain direct, one-on-one communication for special requests and delivery

The solution

I designed a mobile-first, high-accessibility web catalog that prioritizes clarity through bold typography and intuitive navigation. To bridge the gap between digital browsing and manual ordering, I implemented a hybrid checkout system:

  1. WhatsApp Integration: Instead of a complex checkout process, the site generates a pre-formatted list that the user sends directly to the vendor via WhatsApp. This preserved the close contact customers valued while streamlining the selection process.
  2. Inclusive Design: I included a simplified "List View" specifically for customers who preferred the original text-based format, ensuring no user was left behind.
  3. Operational Efficiency: A simplified Wordpress admin panel allows for quick real-time updates to pricing and stock, ensuring customers always have accurate information.

This approach provided a professional digital presence that improved product discovery without sacrificing the brand’s personal connection.
