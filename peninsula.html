Peninsula Hotels Customization

While the Synxis Booking Engine is a powerful platform, luxury brands like The Peninsula Hotels & Resorts require a digital experience that transcends 'out-of-the-box' functionality. During my tenure, I became the lead specialist for these high-profile customizations, with the Peninsula project serving as the final milestone of my work in this capacity.

Project Overview

Peninsula Hotels & Resorts required a comprehensive overhaul of their booking experience to align with their new brand identity, while including key content areas to provide guest information and improve the booking experience.

  • Role: Frontend Developer & Technical Consultant
  • Technologies: JavaScript, CSS, HTML5
  • Duration: 2 months
  • Year: 2025

The Challenge

The core application code and structure remained immutable. To achieve the desired UI/UX, an additional layer was included using only external HTML, CSS and JavaScript. This required a deep understanding of the booking engine's functionality and DOM structure to ensure all customizations were safe and adapt to future platform updates.

The Process

Acting as both a developer and a technical advisor, I bridged the gap between high-fidelity creative visions and technical feasibility.

Using the client's prototypes as a baseline, I conducted a comprehensive feasibility analysis, identifying potential constraints and proposing alternatives. I offered expert guidance on UI/UX best practices and provided creative workarounds for technical limitations.

The Solution

I created a standard setup based on their brand identity, translating their website styling to a booking engine theme. With this in place, it allowed me to add the customization layer to complete the customer's vision, which included the following key features:

JavaScript-driven Enhancements: hero system that delivered imagery and promotional content tailored to specific hotel properties. Bespoke modules for online booking benefits and direct-assistance contact

Custom Feature Integration: custom display layer to showcase high-tier suites not available for direct online booking.

System Refinement: refined global interactive elements, typography, and header for a seamless, high-end feel.
